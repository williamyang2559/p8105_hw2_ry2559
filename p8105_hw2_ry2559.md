The cleaned subway dataset contains 1868 observations and 19 variables.
It includes station name and line, station latitude and longitude,
routes served, entrance type, entry permission, vending status, and ADA
compliance. I cleaned the data by standardizing variable names,
selecting the requested variables, and converting the entry variable
from YES/NO to a logical variable. These data are not fully tidy because
the routes served are stored across multiple columns (`route1` through
`route11`) rather than in a single route variable.

The combined Trash Wheel dataset contains 1269 observations and 15
variables. The data include information on the date of each dumpster
collection, weight and volume of trash collected, counts of different
types of litter such as plastic bottles, polystyrene, cigarette butts,
glass bottles, plastic bags, wrappers, and sports balls, as well as the
Trash Wheel responsible for each collection. I imported and cleaned the
three Trash Wheel datasets separately, removed non-data rows,
standardized variable names and types, and then combined them into a
single tidy dataset.

For the available data, Professor Trash Wheel collected a total of
294.96 tons of trash. Gwynnda collected 18,120 cigarette butts in June
2022.
